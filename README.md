## Current Research

I am developing numerical methods to investigate how fluid injection energy, particle deformation, capillary forces, and evolving pore-network topology control flow through soft granular materials.

Key themes include:

- Coupled **Discrete Element Method–Pore Network Modeling (DEM–PNM)**
- Two-way hydro-mechanical coupling
- Multiphase flow and viscous/capillary fingering
- Dynamic porosity–permeability relationships
- Reduced-order modeling and machine-learning surrogates
- Minkowski functionals and Betti numbers for pore-topology analysis

## Previous Research

My master's thesis, **“Soil–Structure–Structure Interaction Based on Substructure Configurations,”** used nonlinear three-dimensional LS-DYNA simulations to study fixed-base, SSI, and SSSI responses under earthquake loading in Kathmandu Valley soil.

A paper based on this work was published in the **16th IOE Graduate Conference**.
